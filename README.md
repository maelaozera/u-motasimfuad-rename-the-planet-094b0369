Entry for the make.fun bounty "Rename the Planet": write one line, under 200 characters, that gives a planet in our solar system a funny new name and says why in the same breath. A house judge compares lines two at a time on funniness, whether the why earns the name, freshness of the angle, and tightness of the wording.

Entry:

> Rename Jupiter ‘The Bouncer’ — it takes every asteroid hit so the little planets can party in peace.

Run `node main.ts` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
